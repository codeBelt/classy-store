## Undo / Redo Editor

withHistory() adds undo/redo via snapshot stack. Use pause()/resume() to control recording and useHistoryStatus() to render its status.

**API** · `withHistory(store, options?): HistoryHandle` from `@codebelt/classy-store/utils`.

Use `pause()` to skip recording edits and `resume()` to record subsequent changes.
